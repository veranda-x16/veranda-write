PL.DIC -- the Polish spelling dictionary for Writer
====================================================

300 000 Polish word forms: the inflected forms of Morfologik 2.1
"PoliMorf" (morfologik-polish 2.1.9), lower-cased, that are most frequent
in the FrequencyWords Polish list. Built by tools/fetch_morfologik.sh with
tools/build_dictionary.py; format in docs/dictionary-format.md.
